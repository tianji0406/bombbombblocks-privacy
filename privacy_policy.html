2. Summary

Bomb Bomb Blocks does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game works fully offline. You do not need an account.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your own device, using Android's private app storage: your high-score and time records (score, play time, level, date), your settings (sound, vibration, background color, language-independent options), whether you have seen the tutorial, and your Pro status. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage analytics. The app does not request internet access and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.

5. Permissions

The app uses only the vibration permission, to give optional haptic feedback. You can switch vibration off in the app menu.

6. Purchases and advertising

The current version contains no advertising. If a future version adds an optional purchase (for example "Pro") through Google Play Billing, the payment is processed entirely by Google; we do not receive your payment card or bank details. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before release to describe the ad provider, the data it processes and your choices.

7. Children

The game contains no personal data collection, chat, social features or links, so it can be played without disclosing any information.

8. Your rights
